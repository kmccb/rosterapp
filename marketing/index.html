01 / What it is

The team, in everyone’s pocket.

On a Friday night, the announcer, the visiting coach and half the bleachers are trying to work out who just made the play. The roster exists, but it’s a PDF in someone’s inbox.

Roster puts every team at your school on one web link. Families open it from a QR code or your athletics site, pick a sport, and type a jersey number to see the player.

Lookup
Jersey number in; name, position and class out.
Team
The full roster, searchable by name, number or position. Football also filters by offense, defense and special teams.
Schedule
Every game, home and away: what’s coming up, and what’s been played.
League
Football’s conference standings.
Stats Advanced
Season stats and leaders, from your team’s MaxPreps page.

04 / Getting it to families

One link, everywhere your fans already look.

Take-home flyer

A half-page flyer with the QR code, sent home in backpacks or handed out at registration.

Athletics website

A “Roster & Scores” button on your athletics page. I send the image and the link; your web person pastes it in.

Gate and bleacher signs

A QR sign at the ticket gate, the concession stand and the press box, so visiting fans find it too.

Game program

The QR code on the back of the program, next to the roster page it replaces.

School messaging

One post or text through your parent messaging and the booster accounts before the first home game.

PA announcement

“Want tonight’s roster on your phone? Scan the code on the gate sign or in your program.”

Save it like an app

iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.

Questions athletic directors ask

Which sports?
Any sport with a roster and a schedule, one roster per sport. The demo shows football, volleyball and basketball; soccer, baseball, softball and track are added the same way.
What about student privacy?
The page shows only what’s on the roster you send: number, name, grade and position, plus height and weight if you include them. No photographs. A roster comes down the day your school asks.
Who keeps it updated?
Your sheet does, if you link one. Otherwise, email me the change and I make it.
Does IT need to approve anything?
No. There’s nothing to install on school devices and no student accounts.
What does it cost?
$1,200 a year for every sport at your school, and your first season is free.
