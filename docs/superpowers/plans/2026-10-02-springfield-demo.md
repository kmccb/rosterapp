# The demo becomes Springfield — spec and plan

**Date:** 2026-10-02

## Why

The first prospect is Springfield Local (New Middletown). A video for them already exists
(`docs/design/springfield-video/`, a Claude Design composition): black and orange, the tiger
crest, the Springfield Tigers hub with Football / Volleyball / Basketball, Lookup on #24 Marcus
Bell, Team, Schedule, and League with MVAC Scarlet. The live demo at `/oh/demo/` is still a
fictional "Springfield Local, Springfield" in violet and gold with an SL monogram. An athletic
director who watches the video and opens the demo should see the same school.

Decisions already made with the seller:
- **Real football data.** Football schedule, scores and MVAC Scarlet standings come live from the
  committed directory files (`public/oh/data/<slug>.json`, refreshed Sat+Wed), not baked.
- **Leave out what the app doesn't do.** The video shows player stats on the Lookup card and a
  Region 21 toggle on League; the demo shows neither.

## What ships

- `DEMO_SLUG` becomes the real directory slug `springfield-new-middletown`.
- `public/oh/demo.json` (regenerated by `node scripts/build-demo.mjs`) carries only what a paid
  activation would carry: identity (colors `{ground: '#0e0c0b', accent: '#f26722'}`, the crest),
  `sportNames` = football, volleyball, basketball, sample rosters for each, sample schedules for
  volleyball and basketball (football: `schedule: null` — the directory's), the league
  `{name: 'MVAC Scarlet', members: [...]}`, and **no baked `seasons`** and **no baked weather**.
- MVAC Scarlet members (exact directory slugs): `mcdonald-mcdonald`,
  `jackson-milton-north-jackson`, `campbell-memorial-campbell`, `mineral-ridge-mineral-ridge`,
  `lowellville-lowellville`, `waterloo-atwater`, `western-reserve-berlin-center` (NOT
  `western-reserve-collins`).
- Crest: `docs/design/springfield-video/tigers-logo.png`, resized with `sharp` the same way the
  panel's `resizeLogo` sizes an upload (read `src/oh/look.ts` for the limits) and inlined as a
  data URI. It must stay under the theme ceiling the database enforces for a real upload (500 kB).
- Sample rosters: football uses the video's players verbatim (`tigers-video.jsx` ROSTER and
  PLAYERS: #1 Owen Carter … #7 Tobias Kerr, #2 Jalen Reyes, #24 Marcus Bell RB/LB Senior 5′11″
  195 lb) plus the PDF's #10 Caleb Ostrowski, #11 Nate Pryor, #12 Luke Danner, #14 Isaac
  Moreno, #15 Grant Yoder, filled out to a believable varsity roster (~30) with invented names.
  Volleyball and basketball: invented rosters of believable size. No stats anywhere.
- Volleyball and basketball schedules: invented dates in their real seasons (volleyball Aug–Oct,
  basketball Nov–Feb, consistent with `src/oh/sportSeasons.ts`), against the real MVAC Scarlet
  schools' names. They are baked and may keep using the existing week-shifting so they never go
  stale; football is not shifted (it's live).
- `src/oh/demo.ts`: a demo school no longer has to carry its own baked season (`asDemo` must
  accept a file whose `seasons` is empty/absent); `demoSeason` returns null for anything not baked
  so `store.ts`'s `loadSeason` falls through to the real directory fetch — for the school and for
  every League member.
- `src/oh/store.ts` `loadWeather`: on the demo, use baked weather if present, otherwise fall
  through to the normal `/oh/weather.json` path. Add `springfield-new-middletown` to
  `paid-schools.json` so `scripts/paid-weather.mjs` writes its real kickoff forecast.
- `oh/demo/index.html`: title "Springfield Tigers — a demo", theme-color `#0e0c0b`, description
  updated.
- `School.tsx` demo footer: say plainly what's sample and what's real, e.g. "Sample rosters —
  the football schedule, scores and standings are real." (typographic punctuation).
- Hub/header naming comes from the directory ("Springfield", "New Middletown") — fine; don't add
  a display-name override.

## Constraints

- **Poland must not change**: never edit `src/styles.css`; `npm run build` must end with the
  guard passing (three pages byte-identical, precache 32). Nothing under `src/oh/` imports the root
  app runtime.
- Tests pass env-free; `npx vitest run` and `npx tsc --noEmit` green. `src/oh/demo.test.ts`
  currently asserts every league member has a baked season and that members' dates match the demo
  school's; rewrite those to the new contract (members are NOT baked; the file carries no
  seasons), keeping every assertion that still describes something true (rosters, schedules
  inside their sport's months, identity validates through rosterStore's validators, the crest
  size, the league member slugs exist in `public/oh/index.json`).
- `scripts/build-demo.mjs` keeps its self-checks (it asserts its output before writing); update
  them to the new shape rather than deleting them.
- Comments explain why, in the repo's prose voice. Commit messages are plain sentences saying why,
  ending with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
