# A marketing site for Roster, and a demo that belongs to nobody

2026-10-13

## Why

Roster has a product, a price, a demo and a sales PDF, and no address a
stranger can be sent to. The PDF was made for one prospect and travels by
email. An athletic director who hears about Roster from another school has
nowhere to look.

The site is for that reader: an Ohio athletic director or booster who has
never spoken to Tom. It has done its job when they open the demo and then
send an email.

Because the site is public, the demo it links to stops being a page shown to
one prospect and becomes a page shown to everyone. Today that demo is the
real Springfield (New Middletown) wearing invented players. That was a
deliberate exception for one pitch; it is not something to point the whole
state at. So the demo goes back to a fictional school first, and the site is
built on top of it.

## What was decided

- The site lives at `https://getroster.scottforge.ai`, a separate address
  and a separate Cloudflare Pages project. The bare `roster.scottforge.ai`
  is Poland's installed app and does not move. Moving Poland to its own path
  so the root can sell the product is a possible off-season project and is
  out of scope here.
- It wears scottforge.ai's design.
- Prices are the PDF's: one team $400/season, whole school $1,200/year,
  district custom, first season free.
- Contact is an email link and a demo button. No form, no server piece.
- The sponsor ("Presented by") offer is dropped.
- The site claims only what the app does today: no player stats, no Hudl, no
  Region rankings.
- Schedule copy does not name scheduling vendors. Only ScheduleStar and
  Eventlink sync today; everything else is entered by hand.
- The demo becomes a fictional school.

## Part 1 — the demo becomes fictional again

This lands first, because the site's screenshots are taken from it.

### The school

`/oh/demo/` shows **Harlow Valley**, a school that does not exist, slug
`harlow-valley-demo`. Not "Springfield Local", the name the earlier fictional
demo used: that is the real name of the prospect's district.

Its conference is invented too, reusing the five rivals the earlier
fictional demo had (`ashcombe-demo`, `bellhaven-demo`, `cedar-ridge-demo`,
`elmbrook-demo`, `marlow-central-demo`). Every slug ends in `-demo` so none
can collide with the 717 real files in `public/oh/data`, and
`scripts/build-demo.mjs` refuses to write if one does.

Colors and crest are its own: the violet ground and gold accent the earlier
fictional demo wore (`#2f1466`, `#f5b63d`) and a crest generated by the
script. Nothing of Springfield's — the tiger, the black and orange, #24
Marcus Bell — stays.

### What it carries

Commit `f5eb0d5` turned a self-contained fictional demo into one that reads
Springfield's real football from the directory. This reverses that
direction, keeping what was learned since:

- **Football is baked again.** A fictional school has no directory file, so
  `demo.json` carries its season, the five rivals' seasons, and a conference
  whose standings agree with the games behind them. `src/oh/store.ts` and
  `src/oh/demo.ts` answer the season, the League tab's member seasons and
  the kickoff forecast out of that file, as they did before `f5eb0d5`.
- **Three sports**, as now: Football, Volleyball, Basketball. The hub, the
  per-sport shift of pasted schedules within their own months (`582ec8e`),
  and the trimmed prospect's-own-page chrome (`9934dde`) are kept.
- **No stats**, as now.
- The footer line becomes plainly "Sample school, players, schedules and
  scores."
- The demo once again needs no signal after it has loaded and asks the
  database for nothing.

### What follows from it

- `springfield-new-middletown` comes out of `paid-schools.json`. Its only
  reason to be there was the demo's forecast.
- `/oh/springfield` and the real Springfield page are untouched.
- `docs/selling.md`'s "What to show a prospect" section is rewritten for the
  fictional school, including the twice-yearly re-run, which now covers
  football as well.
- `src/oh/demo.test.ts` is rewritten to match. It must stay independent of
  cron-refreshed directory data, which `582ec8e` established.
- `CLAUDE.md`'s "Where things stand" paragraph about the demo is corrected.

### What it costs

The Springfield pitch loses a demo that shows the athletic director their
own season. The PDF still carries Springfield's dress. If that pitch needs
its tailored page back, it is a separate address later, not `/oh/demo/`.

## Part 2 — the site

### Where the code lives

A folder `marketing/` at the repo root: `index.html`, `site.css`, an `img/`
folder, and a favicon. Plain files, no build step, no JavaScript beyond what
a details/summary FAQ needs (none).

It sits outside `src/`, `public/` and `dist/`, so neither Vite pass reads
it, the guard does not see it, and Poland's precache cannot pick it up.
Nothing in it imports from the app and nothing in the app imports from it.

### The look

Taken from scottforge.ai's own stylesheet values:

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Ground | `#0C0B09` (coal), `#141210` (ash) for alternate bands |
| Text | `#F2EBDD` (paper), `#D4CBB9` (paper-dim) for body |
| Accent | `#E85D2F` (ember), `#FF8A3D` hover, `#FFC857` for button text |
| Muted | `#8A8276` (fog) |
| Borders | `rgba(242,235,221,.08)`, hot `rgba(232,93,47,.35)` |
| Display | Fraunces, weight 450, tight tracking, ember italic for the stressed word |
| Body | Archivo 400/600 |
| Labels | JetBrains Mono, uppercase, wide tracking |

Sections are numbered the way scottforge.ai numbers them (`01 / WHAT IT
IS`). Buttons are outlined, 3px radius, mono-spaced uppercase with an arrow.
Fonts load from Google Fonts with `display=swap`.

The page must read well at 375px wide and must not scroll sideways. Phone
screenshots sit in a simple CSS frame.

### The page

One page, top to bottom:

1. **Top bar.** "Roster" wordmark, "by ScottForge" linking to scottforge.ai,
   anchor links, and a "Try the demo" button.
2. **Hero.** "Every team. *One link.*" Rosters, schedules and scores for
   every sport at your school, on any phone, with no app to download and no
   account to create. Two buttons: Try the demo, Email Tom. One phone
   screenshot of the Lookup tab.
3. **01 / What it is.** The Friday-night problem from the PDF, then the four
   tabs: Lookup (number in; name, position and class out), Team, Schedule,
   League (conference scores and standings).
4. **02 / A look inside.** Three screenshots — Team, Schedule, League — each
   with a two-line caption. A quiet line says the screens show a sample
   school.
5. **03 / Your setup.** "Two things to share." Roster: a Google Sheet is
   best, a spreadsheet file or a paste also works. Schedule: football comes
   from the statewide directory on its own; for other sports, the calendar
   link from your scheduling service, or Tom enters the schedule. Then "I
   take care of the rest": your colors and crest, cleaning up names and
   numbers, conference scores and standings, hosting, your QR code and
   flyer, changes through the season.
6. **04 / Getting it to families.** The PDF's six: take-home flyer, website
   button, gate and bleacher signs, game program, school messaging, PA
   announcement. Then "Save it like an app" for iPhone and Android.
7. **05 / What it costs.** Three cards — One team $400/season, Whole school
   $1,200/year (marked recommended), District custom — and a pilot card:
   the first season is free. "Every plan includes" lists setup, colors and
   crest, conference standings, hosting and updates, QR code and flyer,
   support by phone or email.
8. **06 / Launch plan.** Day 1 share two links; days 2–4 review your page;
   game week get it out; all season it keeps itself current. Then
   "Questions athletic directors ask": which sports, student privacy, who
   keeps it updated, does IT need to approve anything, what does it cost.
9. **07 / Start here.** Try the demo; `tom@scottforge.ai` as a mailto with
   the subject "Roster for our school"; a line that Roster is built and run
   by one person in Youngstown, linking to scottforge.ai.
10. **Footer.** © 2026 ScottForge, a link to the privacy page
    (`https://roster.scottforge.ai/oh/?privacy`), and the demo link again.

Copy rules: second person, plain sentences, typographic apostrophes and
quotes, "I" for Tom as the PDF and scottforge.ai both do. No claim the app
cannot back today. The privacy answer says what the privacy page says and
no more.

### Pictures

Screenshots are taken from the built fictional demo at 390px wide, saved as
WebP in `marketing/img/`, each with real alt text and explicit width and
height. They are re-taken whenever the demo's look changes; `selling.md`
says so beside the twice-yearly demo re-run.

### Page metadata

Title "Roster — every team at your school, one link". A description, Open
Graph title, description and image, a canonical URL, and `theme-color`
coal. No analytics, no cookies, no beacon.

### Deploying

A second Cloudflare Pages project, connected to the same repository:

- Root directory `marketing`, no build command, output directory `.`
- Build watch path `marketing/**`, so the twice-weekly directory commits do
  not redeploy it
- Custom domain `getroster.scottforge.ai`

Creating the project and attaching the domain are dashboard steps for Tom.
The exact steps go into `README.md`'s Deploying section.

### Documents that change

- `docs/selling.md`: the price line becomes the three tiers, and the site's
  address goes at the top of "What to show a prospect".
- `CLAUDE.md`: a fourth product in the opening list, and the price in the
  paid-tier line.
- `README.md`: the second Pages project.

## What must not happen

- Neither `src/styles.css`, the three guarded pages, the baseline nor the
  precache changes. `npm run build` passes the guard with the baseline
  untouched, before and after both parts.
- `marketing/` gains no dependency on the app's build, and the app's build
  gains none on it.
- The real Springfield page shows no invented player anywhere.

## How it is checked

**Part 1:** `npx vitest run` and `npx tsc --noEmit` clean; `npm run build`
green with the guard; the built `/oh/demo/` opened at phone width with the
network cut after load — hub, all three sports, football's League tab and
the forecast all present.

**Part 2:** the page opened locally at 1280px and 375px; no horizontal
scroll; every link and anchor resolves; headings in order; contrast of body
text and buttons against coal at AA; `npm run build` green once more with
the folder present. After Tom's dashboard steps, the live address is
opened and the same link check repeated.

## Out of scope

- Moving Poland off the root address.
- A contact form, analytics, a blog, more than one page.
- A sponsor slot in the app.
- A tailored per-prospect demo address.
